# Fixed protocol

How do TomTom and 2GIS traffic-aware travel-time estimates diverge on fixed Astana routes during evening peak compared with off-peak periods?

Provider choice follows verified access and publication terms; original A1 Google/Yandex and A2 Google/2GIS remain historical designs.

20 routes rotate in batches of 4 every 15 minutes: nominal per-route revisit 75 minutes; GitHub schedule delays recorded, not backfilled.

48-hour bounded window replaces 14-day A1 plan because of demo quotas; no quota-increasing retries.

ORS-only pilot tests software and evidence workflow; traffic-aware comparison remains unexecuted.

Same cycle and route with timestamp gap at most 60 seconds define a pair, not a calendar-minute bucket.

Length agreement within 5 percent is only a proxy, not proof of matching route geometry.

Scheduled slots are nominal, not guaranteed. No historical traffic is invented or backfilled. The pilot runs three full ORS-only cycles, with five minutes between the end of one cycle and the start of the next. Public route facts and geometries from ORS carry CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution. TomTom and 2GIS adapters exist but remain disabled pending applicable permissions; Google and HERE remain unavailable. The primary comparison cannot be represented as complete.
